Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Riwaaz Entertainment ("we", "us", "our"), based at D, 254, Rameshwar Nagar, Kudi Bhagtasni Housing Board, Basni, Jodhpur, Rajasthan 342005, provides wedding and event planning services. This policy explains what information we collect from visitors and enquirers, and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you contact us through this website, WhatsApp, phone, or in person, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details (phone number, email, city)
  • Event details you share with us (date, venue, guest count, budget, preferences)
  • Messages you send us over WhatsApp or other channels, for the purpose of responding to your enquiry

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and discuss your event requirements
  • To share proposals, quotes, and updates related to your event
  • To coordinate with vendors and venues on your behalf, where relevant to your event
  • To send you service-related messages over WhatsApp or phone, where you have contacted us through that channel

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to your event or enquiry without asking you first.

WhatsApp communication

If you message us on WhatsApp, your conversation is used only to respond to you and manage your enquiry or booking. Standard WhatsApp terms and privacy practices, set by WhatsApp/Meta, also apply to messages sent through that platform.

Data retention

We retain enquiry and event details for as long as reasonably needed to deliver our services and to maintain business records, after which they may be deleted on request.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, at any time by reaching out through the contact details below.
