Wedding Venues in Jodhpur & What They Actually Cost

2026  ·  Riwaaz Entertainment

Picking a venue in Jodhpur usually starts the same way for every couple: falling in love with a palace on Instagram, then hitting a wall trying to figure out what it actually costs. Most venues don't publish pricing, so you end up sending the same enquiry to five different places just to compare. We put together the numbers we usually walk couples through in that first consultation call, so you have a real starting point before you even reach out.

By Guest Count: Which Venues Actually Fit

Intimate weddings (50–150 guests)

Boutique heritage properties like RAAS Jodhpur and Ajit Bhawan Palace are built for this size — smaller courtyards and gardens that don't feel empty with a tighter guest list, without losing the "Jodhpur palace" feeling.

Mid-size weddings (150–350 guests)

Larger heritage hotels with bigger lawns and banquet spaces, like WelcomHeritage Balsamand Lake Palace, hit the sweet spot here — room to breathe without needing a full royal palace buyout.

Grand weddings (350+ guests)

This is Umaid Bhawan Palace territory, and the Mehrangarh Fort grounds for a truly dramatic setting. Expect these to command the top of the cost range — the scale and setting come at a premium.

What It Actually Costs

The numbers below are based on WedMeGood's Indian wedding industry data. Jodhpur destination weddings average somewhere around ₹50–70 lakh — genuinely more accessible than Jaipur (₹73 lakh average) or Udaipur (₹60–80 lakh), while still delivering that unmistakable Rajasthan setting. A full Jodhpur wedding, top to bottom, can run anywhere from roughly ₹40 lakh for a 150-guest heritage-hotel wedding up to ₹5 crore-plus for a full palace takeover.

Broken down by category:

  • Venue — anywhere from ₹15 lakh to over ₹1 crore, plus ₹15–80 lakh for a guest room block, depending on the property
  • Catering — roughly ₹3,000–8,000 per plate at Jodhpur's palaces and heritage hotels
  • Decor — ₹20–50 lakh at the higher end for flowers, lighting, mandap, and furniture
  • Entertainment — ₹5–15 lakh for live bands, DJs, and cultural performers

A Simple Way to Save 20–30%

If your dates are flexible, June through August — Jodhpur's monsoon and Chaturmas period — sees the lowest demand of the year, and venue rates typically drop 20–30% below peak season as a result. The trade-off is fewer traditionally auspicious wedding dates in that window, so it's worth weighing against how important that is to your family.

Picking the Right One for You

Numbers on a page only get you so far — the right venue also depends on your guest list's travel needs, how much you want built-in versus brought-in, and dates that work for your family's calendar. That's usually where the second half of our first call goes.
