What Makes it Different

What Makes a Destination Wedding Different

Multi-City, Multi-Day Coordination

One wedding, several cities, several ceremonies — we build a single itinerary that ties every city and every event together, instead of handling each location as a separate booking.

Palace & Heritage Venues, City by City

Jodhpur's forts and havelis, Udaipur's lakeside palaces, Jaipur's royal venues, Jaisalmer's desert backdrops — each city has its own character, and we help you pick the one that actually fits the wedding you're imagining.

Guest Travel & Accommodation

Out-of-town guests need hotel blocks, transfers, and a clear schedule. We handle that coordination so your family isn't fielding logistics questions during the week of the wedding.

Decor Adapted to Every Venue

The same décor language doesn't work in a desert fort and a lakeside palace. Our styling adapts to each specific venue, not a one-size template.
