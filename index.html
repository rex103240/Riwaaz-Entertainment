Who We Are

Every family has its own riwaaz.

In Hindi, riwaaz means the way things are done — the customs passed down, the rituals that matter. We don't do cookie-cutter events. We sit with your family, understand your riwaaz, and build a celebration around it.

Born in Jodhpur. Built on tradition. Driven by a team that treats every event like their own. From the first phone call to the last guest leaving, we handle the décor, logistics, vendors, and on-ground execution — so the only thing you need to do on the day is show up and celebrate.
